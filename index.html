How I work

I do my best work on systems that have to keep running at 3 AM.

Usually the headline problem is not the problem. Latency, downtime, a team that has slowed down: the cause tends to sit one layer below, in service boundaries, retry storms, or deploy coupling. I would rather find that than ship around it.

I mentored seven junior engineers at Cafebazaar and five were promoted internally. I am not big on force-multiplier framing, but the most useful thing a senior engineer can do is leave a team better at the job than they found it.

Away from the terminal I cook a lot and chase cuisines I have not tried, walk every city I visit deliberately off the tourist map, and keep a collection of useless trivia. Ask me about the connection between clicker lighters and clocks.
